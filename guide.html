Must-Try Local Flavors

Sweet & Pastry

Pistachio Baklava

Crisp layers of golden phyllo pastry filled with crushed Antep pistachios, lightly sweetened with syrup, and served alongside Turkish tea.

Traditional Feast

Charcoal-Grilled Kebabs

Tender spiced meats roasted over open embers and served with warm flatbread, charred tomatoes, and sumac-seasoned onions.

Essential Istanbul Travel Tips

Quick notes stamped from experience before you explore.

1. Get an Istanbulkart Early

Pick up a reloadable transit card at the airport or any tram station—it works on trams, metros, and Bosphorus ferries.

2. Take the Public Ferry at Sunset

Ride the commuter ferry between Eminönü and Kadıköy for the best skyline views between Europe and Asia for just a few lira.

3. Dress Modestly for Mosques

Bring a light scarf and wear knee-length clothing when visiting the Blue Mosque or Hagia Sophia, and avoid main prayer times.

4. Explore Side Streets Around Bazaars

Step one or two alleys away from the main Grand Bazaar gates to find authentic, quieter tea courtyards and artisan workshops.
