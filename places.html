Places to Visit in Istanbul

Three iconic landmarks stamped from the heart of the city.

Historic Landmark

The Blue Mosque

41.0054° N, 28.9768° E

Famous for its six slender minarets and over 20,000 hand-painted blue Iznik tiles lining the interior dome. Visit early in the morning for peaceful courtyard views.

Architectural Marvel

Hagia Sophia

41.0086° N, 28.9802° E

Standing directly across from the Blue Mosque, this towering masterpiece showcases centuries of history, massive floating domes, and intricate calligraphy medallions.

Panoramic Viewpoint

Galata Tower

41.0256° N, 28.9741° E

A medieval stone watchtower rising above Karaköy, offering sweeping 360-degree views across the Golden Horn and the Bosphorus Strait at sunset.
